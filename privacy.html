Privacy

Your answers stay in your browser. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking.

Your answers

  • Your result is worked out in your own browser.
  • Your answers are not sent to this site or to anyone else.

What is saved on your device

  • The quiz you are in the middle of, including your answers, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Which statements you have already seen, so the next run feels different.
  • The topics you picked and your name setting (neutral or gender-specific).
  • All of this stays in your browser's local storage. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Outside services

  • Google Fonts: every page loads its fonts from Google, so Google receives your IP address.
  • YouTube thumbnails: results with music show thumbnails loaded from YouTube, so YouTube receives your IP address too.
  • Links: YouTube and the Aesthetics Wiki only open if you click a link to them. Their own privacy rules apply after that.
  • Hosting: like any website, this one runs on a hosting company's servers, which receive your IP address when they deliver the pages.

Sharing

  • Nothing about your result is shared unless you share it yourself.
  • Share links contain a code for your run or your result, so anyone who has the link can use it.
